Restate your last message. Stop using jargon and speak coherently. State it more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another. Make it easy to understand and grasp.

Make the rewrite ADHD-friendly and accessible:
- Lead with the main point or next action.
- Keep it to a few short sentences or bullets unless essential context needs more.
- Use short paragraphs, clear spacing, and brief bullets for multiple points.
- Use bold sparingly for key takeaways; add headings only when they help scanning.
- Avoid walls of text, nested lists, and decorative formatting.
- Preserve important meaning and caveats; keep the tone friendly, not patronizing.
